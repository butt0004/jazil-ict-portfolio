Why IT?

Why I chose ICT

I chose ICT because I am interested in technology and I enjoy learning how computers and software work. I think ICT is a good field for me because it gives me the opportunity to learn new skills and solve problems.

I also want to keep learning because technology is always changing. I enjoy learning new things and I want to improve my skills step by step during my ICT studies.

What I want to learn

I want to improve my programming, web development and database skills. I also want to learn how to build useful websites and applications.

During my study I want to become more confident at analysing problems, writing maintainable code and presenting my work clearly.

My ambition

My ambition is to become a skilled ICT professional who can build useful digital solutions and work effectively with other people.
