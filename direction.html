My Direction in ICT

At this stage of my study I am exploring the different possibilities within ICT. I am especially interested in software and web development because they combine logical thinking with creativity.

I also want to learn more about databases, cybersecurity, cloud technology and professional software development. By working on different projects, I can discover which area fits me best.

The ICT field of work

ICT is a broad field. Professionals can develop software, build websites, manage systems, analyse data, protect organisations from cyber threats or work with cloud platforms. Good ICT professionals also need communication, teamwork and the ability to keep learning.

Next step

My next step is to gain more practical experience through study projects and use that experience to make a better-informed career choice.
